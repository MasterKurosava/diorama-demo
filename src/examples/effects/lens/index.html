Plate XII · Admiralty chart 1871

The harbour at low water

Engraved on copper by J. Walker and sons, the sheet records every sounding taken by the survey ship Rifleman over the winter of 1870. Depths are given in fathoms at low water of ordinary spring tides; the small figures with a bar beneath them dry at that state of the tide. The wreck of the brig Alma lies north of the outer buoy, marked with its mast above water.

Bearings are magnetic. Variation 19°40′ W in 1871, decreasing about 7′ annually. Heights of hills are in feet above high water. The anchorage shown in the inner road is not recommended in southerly gales.
