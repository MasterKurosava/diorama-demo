---
code: RENDER_LOST
level: error
message: 'the GPU device was lost'
summary: The GPU device or WebGL context was lost, or rendering was disabled, so every effect switched off and the DOM shows again.
---

## Why it happens

- WebGPU reports a lost device through `renderer.onDeviceLost`, for example after a driver reset or a crash of the browser's GPU process.
- The canvas fires `webglcontextlost` on the WebGL 2 backend. Browsers drop contexts under memory pressure, after a driver reset, or when a page creates too many WebGL contexts.
- The kernel disabled the `render` system after it failed 10 frames in a row and reported `SYSTEM_DISABLED`.

The report prints once. In the same frame every instance becomes `unavailable`, its GL objects, textures and anchors are freed, and the page shows its DOM. `app.effects.status` becomes `unavailable`, and effects stay off until the page reloads.

## How to fix

<Steps>

1. Look above this error in the console. If `SYSTEM_DISABLED` came first, fix the first error reported for the `render` system, under the code `SYSTEM`.
2. Count the canvases with a WebGL context on the page, including those of other libraries, and remove the ones you do not need.
3. Make sure the page reads well as plain DOM, since that is what visitors see after a loss.

</Steps>
