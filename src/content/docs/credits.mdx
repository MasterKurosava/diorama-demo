The examples on this site use pictures, models and sound made by others. Each one listed here is in the public domain, released under CC0, or under the SIL Open Font License. Most of them first appeared in the Diorama demo sites, which are fictional: their shops, hotel, festival and studio do not exist, and neither do the brands printed on the renders. Pictures were cropped, resized, toned and re-encoded for the web. No institution or author on this page endorses Diorama.
