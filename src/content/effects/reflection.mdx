## Recipes

### A product cutout on a glossy shelf

For e-commerce product shots, a transparent PNG or WebP gets a reflection below it without baking one into the image. Leave room under the image with a margin: the reflection draws below the element's box and never shifts the layout.

```html
<img
  src="/shop/speaker.png"
  data-dio="reflection"
  data-dio-shadow="0.35"
  style="margin-bottom: 160px"
/>
```

### A phone mockup on dark glass

A lower `strength`, a short `reach` and a little `blur` read as dark glass.

```html
<img
  src="/app/phone.png"
  data-dio="reflection"
  data-dio-strength="0.25"
  data-dio-reach="0.3"
  data-dio-blur="4"
/>
```

### A photo over still water

On a full photo the bottom edge is mirrored; `ripple` adds a gentle wave, as over water.

```html
<img
  src="/stays/boathouse.avif"
  data-dio="reflection"
  data-dio-strength="0.6"
  data-dio-reach="0.6"
  data-dio-ripple="6"
/>
```

## Notes

The reflection takes no clicks and adds nothing to the page layout; give the image room below with a margin. Under reduced motion the ripple stands still.
