## Recipes

### A carton from its front and one side

Most packaging is printed from a flat dieline, so the pictures already exist: export each face as its own image. The front image is the front of the box and sets its width and height. A side picture sets the depth by its own proportions: a side 240 px wide next to an 800 px tall front makes a box 0.3 of its height deep. Faces you leave out borrow from their pair, or take the color of the matching edge of the front.

```html
<img
  src="/shop/tea-front.webp"
  width="600"
  height="800"
  data-dio="box"
  data-dio-right="/shop/tea-side.webp"
  data-dio-top="/shop/tea-top.webp"
  data-dio-back="/shop/tea-back.webp"
  data-dio-yaw="20"
/>
```

### A gift box that lifts its lid

`motion="lift"` raises a lid that covers the top fifth of the box. Hover opens it by `open`, a click or Enter all the way. `inside` colors the bare board inside, which shows once the lid is off.

### A sleeve with a tray

`motion="drawer"` slides a tray out of its sleeve to the right, as on a box of chocolates or a phone. `motion="flap"` swings back the top flap of a folding carton instead.

### Real proportions from the size

Without a side picture `depth` is `auto` and comes out at 0.3 of the width; give it as a share of the front width instead: a box 18 × 24 × 6 cm has a front 18 cm wide, so `depth` is 6 / 18, about 0.33.

```html
<img
  src="/games/harbor-front.webp"
  width="800"
  height="800"
  data-dio="box"
  data-dio-depth="0.25"
  data-dio-spin="18"
/>
```

## Notes

At rest the front is drawn exactly where the image is, so nothing jumps when the engine takes over, and the plain image with its `alt` text stays for screen readers and without WebGL.

The box draws only while it moves: a hover, a drag, the lean, the lid or the idle `spin`. The faces are flat pictures with a simple light, so a box costs its pictures and nothing else to download.

A box inside a link keeps the link's click; it still opens on hover and turns under a drag. On touch screens a tap opens and closes it, and a finger turns it only when the image has `touch-action: pan-y` or `none`.

`motion` and the face pictures rebuild the effect; every other param changes live.
