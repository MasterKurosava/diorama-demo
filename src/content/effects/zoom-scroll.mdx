## Recipes

### Scroll into the picture

The classic travel or real estate hero: a tall section whose stage sticks to the top at the height of the screen, with the picture in a box on it. Between `start` and `end` of the pin the picture grows from its box to the stage. With `sizes="100vw"` the browser picks a source sharp enough for the full screen.

```html
<section style="height: 300vh">
  <div style="position: sticky; top: 0; height: 100vh; display: grid; place-items: center">
    <img
      src="/img/coast-1920.avif"
      srcset="/img/coast-960.avif 960w, /img/coast-1920.avif 1920w"
      sizes="100vw"
      style="width: 40vw; aspect-ratio: 4 / 3; object-fit: cover; border-radius: 24px"
      data-dio="zoom-scroll"
    />
  </div>
</section>
```

The effect finds the sticky stage on its own. For another layout, mark the tall section with `data-dio-section`.

### A window that opens onto the scene

`style="mask"` keeps the picture still at its full-screen framing and opens a window onto it: the scene seems to be there all along, behind the page. `zoom` sets how far it is zoomed in while the window is small.

```html
<img
  src="/img/coast.avif"
  style="object-fit: cover; border-radius: 32px"
  data-dio="zoom-scroll"
  data-dio-style="mask"
  data-dio-zoom="1.3"
/>
```

### Fly through into the next chapter

`style="through"` grows the picture, flies past the screen and fades it out to show what lies behind on the stage, such as the next heading.

```html
<img
  src="/img/coast.avif"
  style="object-fit: cover"
  data-dio="zoom-scroll"
  data-dio-style="through"
  data-dio-end="0.8"
/>
```

## Notes

Before the pin and under reduced motion the picture is the plain `<img>` in its box. Scrolling back plays the zoom backward; `reverse` starts it at full screen and ends it in the box.

With Lenis the scroll is already smooth: keep `ease` at 0. With native scroll a small `ease` such as 0.1 hides the steps of a mouse wheel.

`style` rebuilds the effect; `start`, `end`, `zoom`, `radius`, `reverse` and `ease` change live.
