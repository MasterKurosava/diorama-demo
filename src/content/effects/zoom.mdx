## Recipes

### Condition photos under a loupe

A shop selling used or handmade goods shows the scratches and the stitching up close. In `lens` mode a round loupe follows the pointer and the rest of the photo stays in view. Each image lists its large file in `srcset`, so no extra attribute is needed.

<Example id="effects/zoom/loupe" description="Three photos of a vintage camera in a row, each showing a round magnifying loupe under the pointer." aspect="16 / 7" show={['html']} controls={[]} variants={[]} />

### An old map that zooms on click

On a page that is mostly read, a zoom that follows every pass of the pointer gets in the way. With `trigger="click"` the view opens where the reader clicks and closes on the next click or when the pointer leaves. A `zoom-in` cursor tells the reader it is there.

### A view beside the photo

`side` mode draws the magnified view into another element, as large shops do next to the product photo, and marks the area on the photo. Give the element a size, leave it empty and hide it from screen readers: it holds only the picture.

```html
<div class="product">
  <img
    src="/p/bag-900.avif"
    srcset="/p/bag-900.avif 900w, /p/bag-2400.avif 2400w"
    sizes="50vw"
    data-dio="zoom"
    data-dio-mode="side"
    data-dio-into="#bag-zoom"
  />
  <div class="details">
    <div id="bag-zoom" aria-hidden="true"></div>
  </div>
</div>
```

### Zoom and a lightbox for touch and keyboards

The zoom works for a mouse or a pen only. Wrap each photo in a link to its large file and put `lightbox` on the list: a click or Enter opens the photo full screen, and inside the viewer a pinch or a double tap zooms it on a touch screen. Keep `trigger="hover"` here, since a click already opens the viewer.

```html
<ul data-dio="lightbox">
  <li>
    <a href="/p/front-2400.webp">
      <img
        src="/p/front-900.webp"
        data-dio="zoom"
        data-dio-mode="lens"
      />
    </a>
  </li>
  <li>
    <a href="/p/back-2400.webp">
      <img
        src="/p/back-900.webp"
        data-dio="zoom"
        data-dio-mode="lens"
      />
    </a>
  </li>
</ul>
```

## Notes

The large file is found in this order: `full`, the `href` of a link around the image when it points to an image file, the widest `srcset` candidate, then `currentSrc`. It loads on the first hover and stays for 10 s after the pointer leaves, then its texture is freed and the bytes stay in the cache. A 2400 px picture takes about 31 MB of GPU memory while it is held.

Keep the large file at 2400 to 4000 px on the long side. `resolution` caps the decode at 4096 px, and low and medium quality tiers cap it lower, so a bigger file costs download time and gains nothing on most devices.

The image and its `alt` do not change, and touch screens never load the effect. Pair it with `lightbox` where readers on phones need the detail too.
