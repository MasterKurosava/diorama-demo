## Recipes

### A record that spins while its track plays

`trigger="play"` links the record to a player on the page: it comes out and spins while the player plays, and goes back when it pauses. A click or Enter on the cover plays and pauses it. Name the player with `for`, or put it next to the image. The player keeps its own controls, so the page works without the effect.

### Colored vinyl and a CD

`style="color"` presses the record in `color`, for a limited edition; `style="picture"` prints the cover or the `label` image over the whole disc; `style="cd"` shows a silver compact disc with the rainbow of its tracks. `label` takes the center label as an image or a color.

### A record that comes out in view

`trigger="view"` slides the record out once the cover is well inside the viewport, and it stays out, spinning while it is in view. It suits a release page with one big cover.

## Notes

At rest the sleeve is drawn exactly where the cover is, so nothing jumps when the engine takes over. The record comes out to the right by `out` of its diameter: leave room for it there, or it covers what sits next to the cover.

A record that only spins draws 30 frames a second; while it slides it draws at the full rate, and at rest nothing. Under reduced motion it comes out and goes back at once and never spins.

`style`, `trigger`, `for` and `label` rebuild the effect; `color`, `out`, `rpm`, `tilt` and `shadow` change live.
