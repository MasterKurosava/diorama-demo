## Recipes

### A hero shot that catches the light

For cars, watches, jewelry or any photo that should feel lit, the flare follows the pointer, and `rest` keeps a little of it when the pointer is away.

```html
<img
  src="/cars/coupe.avif"
  data-dio="flare"
  data-dio-streak="0.4"
  data-dio-rest="0.3"
/>
```

### Light sliding over a product as the page scrolls

`style="sweep"` draws a glossy band instead of a lens flare; with `by="scroll"` it slides across as the image passes through the viewport.

```html
<img
  src="/watch/case.avif"
  data-dio="flare"
  data-dio-style="sweep"
  data-dio-by="scroll"
  data-dio-travel="1.2"
  data-dio-glow="0.1"
/>
```

### A low sun in the corner

With `by="fixed"` the light stays where `position` puts it, for a landscape that should feel shot into the sun.

```html
<img
  src="/trips/ridge.avif"
  data-dio="flare"
  data-dio-by="fixed"
  data-dio-position="0.85 0.15"
  data-dio-ghosts="6"
/>
```

## Notes

Keep the flare subtle on photos with text or faces. Under reduced motion the pointer light appears and moves without fades or smoothing, and with `by="scroll"` it stays at `position`.

`style` and `by` rebuild the effect; the other params change live.
