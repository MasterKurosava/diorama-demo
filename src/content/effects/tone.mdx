## Recipes

### Room cards in the brand's colors

Photos from different shoots look like one set once they share two colors. Give every card the brand's ink and paper, and `reveal="fade"` brings the real color back under the pointer, so the reader still sees what the room looks like before booking it.

<Example id="effects/tone/rooms" description="Three hotel room photos in navy and cream that turn back to color under the pointer." aspect="16 / 9" show={['html']} controls={[]} variants={[]} />

### Light dots on a dark site

On a dark page, make `color` the light one: the dots are then light on a dark ground and grow in the highlights, like a screen print on black paper. A lineup of acts photographed by different people reads as one poster.

<Example id="effects/tone/stages" description="Two instrument photos as light blue halftone dots on dark navy, as on a festival lineup." aspect="16 / 10" show={['html']} controls={[]} variants={[]} />

### Team photos that turn to color on phones

`fade` and `spot` need a pointer that hovers, so on a phone the photos would stay toned. `reveal="view"` brings the color back while a photo crosses the middle half of the viewport, as the reader scrolls a team page.

```html
<img
  src="/team/leo.avif"
  data-dio="tone"
  data-dio-color="#1d2a3a"
  data-dio-background="#f4e3c3"
  data-dio-reveal="view"
/>
```

### The same look without WebGL

Before the engine loads, and on devices without WebGL, the photo shows in color. A CSS filter gives a close black and white for the default look; the copy draws over it once it runs.

```css
img[data-dio~="tone"] {
  filter: grayscale(1);
}
```

## Notes

The tone changes how a photo looks, not what it says: its `alt` stays the same. Do not use it on pictures that carry information in their color, such as charts, maps or the color choices of a product.

For halftone, keep `cell` at 4 px or more. Smaller dots fall between the pixels of a standard screen and show a moiré pattern.

`style` and `reveal` rebuild the effect; the colors, `strength` and the grid change live, so a theme switch can write `color` and `background` from code.
