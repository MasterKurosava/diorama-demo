## Recipes

### A field that lights up while you type

With `motion="hover"` the beam runs while the pointer is over the element or focus is inside it, so a wrapped email field keeps its beam while someone types. Cool colors suit a form; a shorter `length` keeps the beam tidy on a short pill.

## Notes

The element stays DOM: the beam draws over its edge on the canvas, as a thin frame around the box, so the inside of a large card costs nothing. It follows the largest of the element's corner radii, so give all four corners the same `border-radius`. The line sits inside the edge, where a 1 or 2 px CSS border is, and the glow spreads to both sides of it.

`motion="loop"` and `motion="pulse"` draw at most `fps` frames a second while the element is on screen and nothing while it is off screen or the tab is hidden; `fps="0"` holds the beam where it is. With `motion="hover"` nothing is drawn at rest.

Under reduced motion the beam stands still as a faint border in its two colors.

`motion` rebuilds the effect when it changes; the other params change live.
