## Recipes

### A lineup that glitches under the pointer

On a festival, music or gaming site, each act or title gets a short burst as the pointer enters its card, and the card is calm the rest of the time. The `slice` style shifts whole blocks and smears a few into streaks. Give each card its own `seed` so no two glitch alike.

### A product film on worn tape

The effect runs on a playing `<video>` as well as on an image. The `vhs` style wobbles the frame, washes out the color and rolls a band of bad tracking down the picture. With `trigger="interval"` the burst comes back every few seconds, at slightly uneven times, while the video is on screen.

### A hero that breaks up with scroll speed

With `trigger="scroll"` the glitch follows the scroll speed: a slow read leaves the image alone, a fast flick tears it apart for a moment. It suits a streetwear drop or a launch page where the hero should feel loud.

```html
<img
  src="/drop/hero.avif"
  data-dio="glitch"
  data-dio-trigger="scroll"
  data-dio-style="slice"
  data-dio-strength="1.5"
/>
```

## Notes

The glitch changes how the image looks for a fraction of a second; its `alt` stays the same. Keep bursts short and rare on pages people read: three flashes or more per second can trigger seizures, so `duration` stays under a second and `interval` above two seconds unless the glitch is small.

Under reduced motion the effect does not run at all and the image shows as it is.

`style` and `trigger` rebuild the effect; `strength`, `blocks`, `split`, `interval`, `duration` and `seed` change live.
