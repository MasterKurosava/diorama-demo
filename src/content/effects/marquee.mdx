## Recipes

### A logo row under the hero

Lay the logos out as one flex row with a `gap`, give each item `flex: none` so it keeps its width, and clip the row with `overflow: hidden`. The effect repeats the row with the same gap between copies, so you write each logo once. Inline SVGs keep their colors from your CSS, and `opacity` or a `filter: grayscale(1)` on a logo carries over too.

```html
<ul class="logos" data-dio="marquee">
  <li><img src="/logos/northwind.svg" /></li>
  <li><img src="/logos/halcyon.svg" /></li>
  <li><img src="/logos/vertex.svg" /></li>
</ul>
```

### A big ticker that turns with the scroll

Agency and event sites often run one huge line across the page. `data-dio-reverse="true"` turns it around when the visitor scrolls up, and a larger `skew` makes it lean harder while the page moves. Scroll past this example to see it.

### Moving only while the page scrolls

With `data-dio-speed="0"` the row stands still until the visitor scrolls, and `boost` sets how far it travels per pixel of scroll. It reads as a part of the scroll rather than an animation of its own, and it draws no frames while the page rests.

```html
<p
  class="band"
  data-dio="marquee"
  data-dio-speed="0"
  data-dio-boost="0.6"
  data-dio-direction="right"
>Design · Build · Ship · Repeat ·</p>
```

## Notes

The content stays in the page once, as you wrote it: screen readers and search engines read it once, and a CSS filter hides it while the copies run. Links inside stay where the page lays them out rather than where the copies move, so for a row of links keep `pause` on: the row slows to a stop under the mouse or keyboard focus, which also gives visitors a way to stop moving content.

The effect draws backgrounds, borders and corner radii of the items in the row, images with their `object-fit`, inline SVGs and text. It does not draw shadows, gradients or background images of the items, and the element's own background stays put as a plain color under the moving content. An image from another origin needs CORS, or the row stays as it is with a warning in development.

The gap between copies is the row's own `column-gap` for a flex or grid row and 1em for plain text, plus `gap`. Under reduced motion the row stands still, filled with copies, and scrolling does not move it. Off screen it draws no frames, and on screen it draws at most 60 a second.

`direction` rebuilds the effect when it changes; the other params change live.
