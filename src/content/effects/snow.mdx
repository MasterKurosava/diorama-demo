## Recipes

### Snow over the whole page for the holidays

Put the effect on `<body>` and the snow falls over the viewport on every page, over the content, and never blocks a click. Keep `count` modest on a page people read, and turn it off after the campaign by removing one attribute.

```html
<body
  data-dio="snow"
  data-dio-count="120"
  data-dio-wind="-0.3"
>
  <main>…</main>
</body>
```

### Cherry petals for a spring collection

`style="petals"` lets pink petals tumble and flip on their way down, for spring launches, weddings and spas. A little `wind` carries them across.

### Autumn leaves in brand colors

`style="leaves"` drops pointed leaves with a darker midrib. Pass your own colors to `color`, `var()` included, to match a seasonal menu or a sale.

```html
<section
  class="autumn"
  data-dio="snow"
  data-dio-style="leaves"
  data-dio-color="#c2410c #f59e0b var(--brand)"
>
  <h2>The autumn menu</h2>
</section>
```

## Notes

The flakes draw over everything in the element's box, or over the viewport on `<body>`, and the element itself is never hidden or changed, so links and buttons under the snow keep working.

Far flakes are small, slow and faint and near ones larger, faster and softer, so the snowfall has depth. With `pile` the drift starts low and builds up over half a minute while the element is on screen; it is drawn for the snow style only.

It draws up to `fps` frames a second while the element is on screen and none off screen; on the low tier at most 70 flakes at 24 frames a second. Under reduced motion it does not load at all.

`style`, `color` and `seed` rebuild the effect when they change. The count, size, speed, wind, drift and push change live.
