## Recipes

### The featured plan on hover

On a pricing table, the plan you recommend can catch the light as the pointer comes over it. A narrower `width` and a lower `strength` keep the band subtle on a large card.

### A badge that glints as it shows up

The `view` trigger sweeps once the first time half of the element is in view. A warm `color` and a stronger `strength` suit a gold member badge or an award seal.

## Notes

The element stays DOM: the band draws over it on the canvas only during a sweep and is clipped to the element's own `border-radius`, corner by corner, so pill buttons work too. A parent with `overflow: hidden` does not clip it, so keep the element fully inside such a parent. Between sweeps nothing is drawn, and the `interval` trigger waits on a timer without drawing frames. Timed sweeps pause while the element is off screen or the tab is hidden.

Keyboard focus on the element or a control inside it sweeps like a hover, and a tap sweeps on touch screens.

Under reduced motion neither the module nor the engine loads for it, and the element stays as it is.

`trigger` rebuilds the effect when it changes; the other params change live and apply to the next sweep.
