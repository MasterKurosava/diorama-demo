## Recipes

### A trip on the table

For a travel story, an event recap or a studio's "about us" page, the photos lie scattered at seeded angles. The visitor drags one to the top or clicks it to hold it up large. With `tabindex` and an `aria-label` the stack is reachable from the keyboard too.

```html
<section
  class="trip"
  data-dio="polaroids"
  tabindex="0"
  aria-label="Photos from the coast. Arrow keys bring the next one to the top, Enter holds it up."
>
  <img src="/trip/1.avif" />
  <img src="/trip/2.avif" />
  <img src="/trip/3.avif" />
</section>
```

### A pinboard of linked projects

`style="grid"` keeps each print over its own place in the layout, slightly turned, like a pinboard; links inside the prints stay links.

```html
<ul
  class="board"
  data-dio="polaroids"
  data-dio-style="grid"
  data-dio-rotate="4"
>
  <li>
    <a href="/work/kiln">
      <img src="/work/kiln.avif" />
    </a>
  </li>
  <li>
    <a href="/work/loom">
      <img src="/work/loom.avif" />
    </a>
  </li>
</ul>
```

## Notes

The pictures stay plain `<img>` elements with their `alt` text, so the stack reads as a list of photos without the effect. Under reduced motion the prints move at once, without easing; a print you carry still follows the pointer.

`style` rebuilds the effect; the other params change live.
