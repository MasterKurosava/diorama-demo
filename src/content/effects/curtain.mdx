## Recipes

### A case study cover

The cover opens from behind a curtain as the visitor reaches it, and closes again on the way back. A curtain in the color of the page reads as the page itself opening.

```html
<img src="/img/cover.avif" alt="Harbour at dawn" style="width: 100%; aspect-ratio: 16 / 9; object-fit: cover" data-dio="curtain" data-dio-color="#f4f1ea" />
```

### A chapter opening in a pinned section

With `by="pin"` the curtain follows the pinned section. `out` closes it again before the section lets go, so the next chapter starts from a closed curtain.

```html
<section style="height: 250vh">
  <div style="position: sticky; top: 0; height: 100vh">
    <img src="/img/valley.avif" alt="The valley at dusk" style="width: 100%; height: 100%; object-fit: cover" data-dio="curtain" data-dio-by="pin" data-dio-style="split" data-dio-end="0.5" data-dio-out="0.85" />
  </div>
</section>
```

`split` parts the halves sideways like a theater curtain by default; `direction="up"` or `"down"` parts them up and down instead.

### From the plan to the photo

`src` makes a second picture the curtain, framed like the image: a plan, a sketch, a title card or a brand pattern.

```html
<img src="/img/terrace.avif" alt="The terrace above the sea" style="object-fit: cover" data-dio="curtain" data-dio-style="liquid" data-dio-src="/img/ground-floor.webp" />
```

### Driven by GSAP

`by="manual"` leaves the opening to `position`, for a timeline or a button.

```ts
const cover = app.effects.get(document.querySelector('.cover'), 'curtain')
gsap.to(cover.params, { position: 1, duration: 1.2, ease: 'power2.inOut' })
```

## Notes

Unlike `reveal`, which brings a picture in from transparency, `curtain` draws the curtain itself: strips that carry their slice away, halves with folds or a wavy liquid edge, with a soft shadow on the picture. Open, it is the picture as it is.

A picture on the first screen waits behind a veil until the curtain is drawn, so the bare picture never flashes before it closes. Under reduced motion the effect is off and the picture is shown as it is.

`style`, `by`, `direction`, `src` and `seed` rebuild the effect; the others change live.
