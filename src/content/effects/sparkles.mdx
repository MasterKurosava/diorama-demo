## Recipes

### A reward when a level is done

On a learning site for kids, `trigger="view"` bursts once the first time half of the heading is in view. Round `dot` sparkles in a soft color read as confetti for the very young; a larger `spread` lets them fly clear of the text.

## Notes

The element stays DOM and keeps every click: the sparkles draw over the page on the canvas, and the click goes on to the link, button or form. A click with the pointer bursts from where it landed; a keyboard click bursts from the element's edge. Keyboard focus starts the `hover` sparkles too.

Nothing is drawn at rest with `hover`, `click` and `view`. With `loop` the sparkles twinkle at most `fps` frames a second while the element is on screen and stop while it is off screen or the tab is hidden, so keep `always` for one or two elements a page.

Under reduced motion neither the module nor the engine loads for it, and the element stays as it is.

`trigger`, `style` and `seed` rebuild the effect when they change; the other params change live.
