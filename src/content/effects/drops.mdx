## Recipes

### A frozen page that thaws

Put `frost` and `drops` on one fixed element over the page. Raise the frost's `cover` to freeze the window; lower it and fire the drops with the same point and duration, and the meltwater appears behind the thaw front and runs down the glass.

```html
<div
  class="window"
  data-dio="frost drops"
  data-dio-frost-cover="0"
  data-dio-drops-motion="thaw"
  data-dio-drops-trigger="manual"
  style="position: fixed; inset: 0; pointer-events: none"
></div>
```

```ts
const pane = document.querySelector('.window')
// Freeze.
pane.dataset.dioFrostCover = '1'
// Thaw from a point, 30% from the left and 60% from the top.
for (const effect of ['frost', 'drops']) {
  pane.setAttribute(`data-dio-${effect}-x`, '0.3')
  pane.setAttribute(`data-dio-${effect}-y`, '0.6')
}
pane.dataset.dioFrostCover = '0'
pane.dataset.dioDropsFire = String(Number(pane.dataset.dioDropsFire ?? 0) + 1)
```

### Condensation on a cold drink

`speed="0"` keeps every drop where it landed and `rate="0"` stops new ones, so the glass is beaded once and the beads evaporate slowly.

```html
<figure
  class="drink"
  data-dio="drops"
  data-dio-rate="0"
  data-dio-count="160"
  data-dio-speed="0"
  data-dio-dry="12"
>
  <img src="/menu/lemonade.avif" alt="Iced lemonade in a tall glass" />
</figure>
```

## Notes

Nothing behind the drops is read: each drop is drawn as a small lens, darker at its top and lit at its bottom, with a crisp rim and one highlight, so it looks like water over any page. The element is never hidden, and clicks go through.

Small drops rest and evaporate after a while; heavy ones run, faster as they grow, swallow the beads in their way and leave a thin trail that dries over `dry` seconds. After a thaw the glass ends clean.

Frames are drawn while drops run or a thaw spreads, at 15 a second while only beads and trails dry, and none once the glass is dry. On the low tier there are fewer drops at 30 frames a second. Under reduced motion it does not load at all.

`motion`, `trigger` and `seed` rebuild the effect; the other params change live.
