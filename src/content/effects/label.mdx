## Recipes

### A wine bottle turned by a drag

`style="bottle"` puts the label on a 750 ml bottle of colored glass with a foil cap. A wine label is narrow, so it covers part of the body, as on a real bottle. `turn="0"` stops the scroll turn and leaves the bottle to a drag and the arrow keys.

### A jar with a colored lid

`style="jar"` is a squat glass jar with a twist lid. `color` is what fills the jar, seen through the glass, and `cap` is the lid. A higher `pitch` shows the top of the lid.

## Notes

Use the flat label as it goes to print, its whole width. The label keeps its proportions: its height takes the label band of the container and its width decides how far around it reaches. A label longer than the body goes once round and gets a lower band. A label with transparent edges, such as a die-cut shape, shows the glass or the metal around it.

Size the image's box for the container and keep the label undistorted with `object-fit: contain`: the container stands in the box as tall as fits. Without WebGL the flat label shows in the same box, all of it readable.

The container draws only while it moves: the scroll with `turn`, a drag, the lean or the idle `spin`. On touch screens the scroll turns it; a finger turns it only when the image has `touch-action: pan-y` or `none`.

`style`, `color` and `cap` rebuild the effect; every other param changes live.
