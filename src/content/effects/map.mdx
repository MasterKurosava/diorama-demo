## Recipes

### A store locator with a visible list

Keep the stores in a list next to the map and point `markers` at its items. Hovering an item lights its marker and a click on a marker follows the item's link. The map itself is `aria-hidden`: the list already says everything it shows.

```html
<div
  class="map"
  data-dio="map"
  data-dio-region="europe"
  data-dio-style="solid"
  data-dio-markers="#stores li"
  aria-hidden="true"
></div>
<ul id="stores">
  <li data-dio-lat="48.86" data-dio-lng="2.35">
    <a href="/stores/paris">Paris</a>
  </li>
  <li data-dio-lat="41.39" data-dio-lng="2.17">
    <a href="/stores/barcelona">Barcelona</a>
  </li>
</ul>
```

### A Pacific view with the sea drawn in

`center` moves the middle of the map east or west, so 150 puts the Pacific in the middle. `sea` draws the sea in `color` at that opacity: faint dots in the dots style, a fill in the solid one.

```html
<div
  class="map"
  data-dio="map"
  data-dio-center="150"
  data-dio-sea="0.2"
  data-dio-color="#3a4a66"
></div>
```

`dio:marker` fires on the map each time a marker lights up or goes dark, with the element of the place in `detail.marker`. Use it for a tooltip or for the address of the store under the pointer.

## Notes

The map is an Equal Earth projection of the Natural Earth land mask (public domain), the same data as `globe`, loaded once for both. The region always fits the box, and a box wider or taller than the region shows more map around it. Antarctica is left out.

It draws only while the arcs draw in, once when the map scrolls into view, and for a few frames as a marker lights up. Under reduced motion the arcs stand drawn and markers light up without easing. Nothing hovers on touch screens, so a tap on a marker works as a click.

Dots take about 12 bytes each, under 150 KB at the default 160 across; `solid` keeps a 260 KB land mask texture on the GPU instead. The `low` tier draws 100 dots across.
