01 — Shadow box

Ridges

A shadow box: the picture cut into seven sheets of paper that stand apart in depth behind a mat. Move the pointer over it to look inside.

How it got here

  1. 01

    One key on both pages

    The card in the landscape and this cover carry the same data-dio-key.

  2. 02

    The 3D lives on

    The canvas is not torn down when the page changes. Nothing reloads, nothing blinks.

  3. 03

    The picture flies

    Its own mesh and texture travel from the card's box to this one, recropped on the way. Not a screenshot.
